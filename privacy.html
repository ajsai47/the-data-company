How we handle what you share.

Draft for review. Last updated: date TBC

What we collect

When you request an appraisal we collect what you type into the form: your name, email, role, fund or company name, and anything you choose to tell us about the company. The quick assessment on the home page runs in your browser; its answers are only sent if you carry them into the appraisal form and submit it.

How we use it

Only to reply to you, prepare a private appraisal, and run a process you have approved. We never sell your information and never share it with a buyer without your consent and a signed NDA.

Where it is kept

Requests are stored in our internal workspace (Notion) and may be sent to us by email. The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when a page loads.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no advertising or analytics cookies.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete what you sent at any time. Email contact email TBC.
