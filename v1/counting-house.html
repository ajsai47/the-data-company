Fig. I — The Mandate

Four acts of representation

I.

Appraisal

What is the corpus worth to the laboratories training frontier models? Rarity, coverage, provenance — priced before it is pitched.

II.

Mandate

You grant exclusive representation. One broker, one process — the archive does not leak across the market.

III.

Negotiation

Qualified buyers, competed against one another under a disciplined process, to the best possible terms.

IV.

Revenue

The license closes; the archive pays. We earn on the outcome, never on the introduction.

Fig. II — A Letter to Data Owners

Every laboratory building a frontier model wants what you have. They will call it partnership, or access, or collaboration — and they will send a procurement team whose entire profession is paying less for it. Nobody in that room is compensated to get you more. That is the seat we sit in.

We are not a marketplace, and we are not a list. We take a small number of archives to market under exclusive mandate, we run the process ourselves, and we are paid on the terms we win — not on making the introduction.

— The Data Company
