Principles

I.

Archives are the keys to the next order of intelligence. Their owners hold a producing asset — the last great asset still sold without representation.

II.

Two worlds meet in this market: those who built the archives, and those who build the machines. Between them stands a wide delta of language, precedent, and power — and this market has a history of taking advantage of its sellers.

III.

We navigate that crossing on the seller's behalf — appraising, negotiating, closing — so that no owner makes the passage alone.

IV.

Though the market is nearly automated, its advantage remains human — judgment, presence, and the handshake, held by people answerable to the seller alone.

Our Oath

Every laboratory building a frontier model wants what you hold — because what you hold is the missing substrate of the next order of intelligence. Human judgment. Ambiguous work resolved well. Years of operational excellence, recorded. That record is the requirement — and you hold the keys.

Our office is simple to state: to win you the highest value and the strongest protections for the assets you hold. We are your broker — we negotiate on your behalf so that you are never taken advantage of, and so that you are rewarded for what your business has built.

And we take one oath, and it runs to the seller alone. We hold nothing of our own to sell, and we answer to no buyer. Every asset class has had its brokers — those who used the client, and those who stood for the client. We swear to be the latter.

Our founder came to this seat by an uncommon road — from building the largest nonprofit community in the world teaching people AI, to working alongside the frontier laboratories on engineering, development, and go-to-market. That work earned the trust of more than a million people. The Data Company exists so that the owners of data are paid their share of what comes next — everyone is making money off the AI revolution, and you should too.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the appraisal cost?

Nothing. A preliminary appraisal is an estimate of the market, offered without obligation. We are paid on outcomes — never on the introduction.

Do you buy data yourselves?

No. We hold no inventory. We represent owners of proprietary archives under exclusive mandate, and our fee runs against the terms we win.

What is an exclusive mandate?

One archive under one representation. Nothing leaks across the market — and every buyer knows the corpus crosses the table once.

What kind of data belongs here?

Proprietary record: human judgment, resolved ambiguity, operational excellence — the corpora laboratories cannot synthesize. Rarity, coverage, and provenance decide the price.

Who sits across the table?

Frontier AI laboratories and foundation-model groups — buyers whose procurement desks are paid to pay less. We sit on the seller's side of that table.

Is my inquiry held in confidence?

Held in confidence, appraised before answered. Your archive enters the market under your mandate and closes under your terms.
