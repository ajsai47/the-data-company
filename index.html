02The problem

Written off is not the same as worth nothing.

When an early-stage company winds down, the cap table goes to zero, but what the company built often stays behind: years of operating records, engineering history, domain-specific datasets, and workflows that took real people real time to create.

AI teams need exactly this kind of real-world data, and most of it sits on a drive with no plan. For a fund, that is unrecovered value on a position already marked down.

03What TDC does

A sell-side advisor for company data.

TDC works for the seller, not the buyer. The seller approves every step, sees every offer, and makes the final call.

01
We work for the seller.

Never the buyer. We assess what a wound-down company has and estimate what it could be worth to AI buyers.

02
Several buyers compete.

We run a competitive process when the seller wants to go ahead, not a sale to the first buyer who asks.

03
We never hold the data.

It stays with the seller until a deal closes. We don't take custody of it, copy it, or warehouse it.

04

No company name reaches a buyer without consent and an NDA. De-identification is built into the process.
