The Data Company — three directions

Three candidate design languages for a private brokerage that represents data owners in exclusive licensing agreements with frontier AI labs. Audience: sellers. Voice: institutional. Each study is a standalone mock landing with its own wordmark, type stack, palette, and component vocabulary.

The Charter

Light · Document-first · classical restraint

Built content-first after the palette exercise: a 19th-c. prospectus as a single-column document — numbered positions, a letter to data owners, the ledger as book of record. Real Bureau of Engraving vignettes with provenance captions. Fraunces + EB Garamond, oxblood seal, near-monochrome.

The Ledger

Light · Editorial-mercantile

Ivory paper, ink, banker's green. Fraunces + Newsreader + Plex Mono. Hairline rules, figure numbers, fleurons, a ruled book of placements. The literary end of the register — a house that publishes its own positions.

Open → B

The Terminal

Dark · Data-native

Near-black, phosphor green, amber marks. Plex Mono leads; Fraunces italic lands the mercantile moments. Status bar, ticker tape, execution path, book of business. The market rendered honestly — data in, dollars out.

The Counting House

Dark · Classical-mercantile

Warm black, parchment, brass. Fraunces display + EB Garamond + gold mono. House seal, roman numerals, a drop-cap letter to data owners, gilt ledger. The Cosmos register tilted from philosophy toward trade.
