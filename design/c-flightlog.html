01 · The problem

Written off is not the same as worth nothing.

When an early-stage company winds down, the cap table goes to zero, but what the company built often stays behind: years of operating records, engineering history, domain-specific datasets, and workflows that took real people real time to create. AI teams need exactly this kind of real-world data, and most of it sits on a drive with no plan.

02 · What TDC does

A sell-side advisor for company data.

TDC works for the seller, not the buyer. The seller approves every step, sees every offer, and makes the final call.

What we doWhat we never do
Represent the seller onlyTake a fee from buyers to steer a deal
Bring several qualified buyers to competeSell to the first buyer who asks
Keep data with the seller until a deal closesTake custody of, copy, or warehouse your data
Require an NDA before any seller name reaches a buyerShare a company's name without consent
Build de-identification into the processSend personal or sensitive records out as-is
