I. The Problem

Written off is not the same as worth nothing.

When an early-stage company winds down, the cap table goes to zero, but what the company built often stays behind: years of operating records, engineering history, domain-specific datasets, and workflows that took real people real time to create.

AI teams need exactly this kind of real-world data, and most of it sits on a drive with no plan.

II. What TDC Does

A sell-side advisor for company data.

TDC works for the seller, not the buyer. The seller approves every step, sees every offer, and makes the final call.

We Do

  • Represent the seller only
  • Bring several qualified buyers to compete
  • Keep data with the seller until a deal closes
  • Require an NDA before any name reaches a buyer
  • Build de-identification into the process

We Never

  • Take a fee from buyers to steer a deal
  • Sell to the first buyer who asks
  • Take custody of, copy, or warehouse your data
  • Share a company's name without consent
  • Send personal or sensitive records out as-is
