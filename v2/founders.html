01Why founders call us

Winding down is hard enough. This part doesn't have to be.

Winding down a company is hard enough. You shouldn't also have to figure out whether the records, code, or datasets your team built have value, or who would pay for them.

TDC does that work for you. We start with a private conversation and a free appraisal. If there's something worth pursuing and you want to go ahead, we take a de-identified summary to several AI buyers under NDA and bring every offer back to you. If you don't like the terms, you walk away. Nothing has been shared that you didn't approve.
