Why it matters to a fund

The mark is zero. The assets may not be.

A write-off closes the position on paper. It doesn't always account for what the company built. Data from real operations is hard for AI teams to get any other way, and the companies that created it are often the last ones to think of it as an asset.

TDC gives you a way to check, company by company, with no cost to find out and no commitment until the seller sees real offers.

One introduction. We handle the rest.

Who to introduce
  • Founders or former officers of portfolio companies that have wound down, been written off, or are clearly heading there
  • Ideally companies in manufacturing, software development, or healthcare, but send anything you're unsure about
  • Whoever still has authority over the company's remaining assets: a founder, board member, or the person handling the wind-down
What we ask of you
  • A short list of candidate companies. Names and a sentence each is enough to start.
  • A warm email introduction to the right founder or officer. The blurb below is ready to forward.

Investor FAQ

Questions funds ask first.

Does this cost the fund anything?

No. The appraisal is free. Fee structure: wording to confirm

Who receives the proceeds?

The seller, according to the company's existing agreements, creditor obligations, and wind-down plan. TDC doesn't change who is owed what.

Does TDC guarantee the company has the right to sell its data?

No. We review what rights appear to exist and flag issues early, but the seller confirms ownership and permissions, and buyers do their own diligence.

Will buyers know which fund or company this is?

Not without consent. Buyers see an anonymized summary first. Names are shared only after the seller approves and the buyer signs an NDA.

What happens to personal or sensitive data?

De-identification is part of the process, not an afterthought. Records that can't be handled responsibly stay out of the deal.
