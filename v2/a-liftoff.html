01 · The problem

Written off is not the same as worth nothing.

When an early-stage company winds down, the cap table goes to zero, but what the company built often stays behind: years of operating records, engineering history, domain-specific datasets, and workflows that took real people real time to create.

AI teams need exactly this kind of real-world data, and most of it sits on a drive with no plan. For a fund, that is unrecovered value on a position already marked down.

02 · What TDC does

A sell-side advisor for company data.

TDC works for the seller, not the buyer. We assess what a wound-down company has, estimate what it could be worth to AI buyers, and run a competitive process when the seller wants to go ahead.

What we doWhat we never do
Represent the seller onlyTake a fee from buyers to steer a deal
Bring several qualified buyers to competeSell to the first buyer who asks
Keep data with the seller until a deal closesTake custody of, copy, or warehouse your data
Require an NDA before any seller name reaches a buyerShare a company's name without consent
Build de-identification into the processSend personal or sensitive records out as-is

04 · Where data is most valuable

Real work, in specialized fields.

I

Manufacturing

Process and quality records, sensor and machine data, inspection imagery, operating procedures.

II

Software development

Code history, issue and review records, test suites, internal tooling and documentation.

III

Healthcare

Operational and workflow data, and de-identified clinical or administrative records where permitted.

Other industries can qualify. The appraisal tells you whether yours does.

05 · How it works

A careful process. Your decision at every step.

Step 01

Assessment call

A short call to understand the company, what it built, and what records remain.

You decide whether to continue.

Step 02

Private appraisal

What the data is, what rights appear to exist, and how AI buyers may value it. Nothing is shared.

You decide whether to go to market.

Step 03

Buyer process

Several buyers review an anonymized summary. Names are shared only under NDA.

You decide which offers to pursue.

Step 04

Seller's final say

See the terms side by side. Accept, negotiate, or walk away.

You decide. Always.
