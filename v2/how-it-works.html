01The four steps

What happens, and who decides.

01
Assessment call

A short conversation, usually with the founder or whoever is handling the wind-down. We learn what the company did, what records remain, and where they're stored. No data is shared on this call.You decide whether to continue

02
Private appraisal

We look at what the data is, how it was created, what permissions and agreements appear to apply, and how AI buyers are likely to see it. You get a written appraisal with a view on fit, likely buyer interest, and anything that needs attention first, such as rights questions or de-identification. The appraisal is private. It is not a listing, and no buyer sees it.You decide whether to take it to market

03
Buyer process

If you go ahead, we prepare an anonymized summary and take it to several qualified AI buyers at once so they compete on price and terms. A buyer learns your company's name only after you approve it and they sign an NDA. Data stays with you; buyers evaluate through approved samples or controlled review, never a handover.You decide which offers to pursue

04
Seller's final say

We bring every offer back to you, laid out side by side: price, license scope, exclusivity, duration, and data-handling terms. We'll give you our recommendation. You accept, negotiate, or walk away.You decide. Always
